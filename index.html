Lily Support

Lily is a private, on-device feeding and diaper tracker for parents and caregivers.

Common questions

Where is my data stored?
On your device. Lily currently has no account or cloud sync.

How do I change units or language?
Open the settings button on the Today screen.

How do I edit an entry?
Open History, select the entry, and choose Edit or Delete.

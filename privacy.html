Lily Privacy Policy

Effective October 19, 2026

Lily stores feeding, diaper, baby-name, preference, and note data locally on your device. Lily does not require an account and does not transmit this information to Cosmic Kettle or third parties.

Data collection

Lily does not collect analytics, advertising identifiers, health data, contact information, location, or usage data. The optional voice and ambient audio included with the app are bundled assets and do not send recordings or microphone data.

Data control

You can edit or delete individual entries in the app. Removing Lily from your device removes its locally stored data, subject to any device backups you control.

Children

Lily is a tool for parents and caregivers. It is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Medical disclaimer

Lily provides descriptive tracking only and is not a medical device or a substitute for professional medical advice.

Contact

For privacy questions, email hello@cosmickettle.com.
